RLC Filter Bench

One series RLC circuit with two outputs. Measured across the resistor it is a band-pass filter. Measured across the inductor and capacitor together it is a notch filter. Move any slider and every graph redraws.

How the graphs are made: the scope traces are the steady-state output, built from the input's Fourier series with every harmonic multiplied by H(jnω). The table under each scope lists the first harmonics. The step response is the inverse Laplace transform of H(s)·A/s, and the pole-zero map plots the roots of LCs² + RCs + 1 and of each numerator. The sound uses Web Audio biquad filters set to the same f₀ and Q.
